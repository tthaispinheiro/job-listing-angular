Challenge FAQs

  • How can I get help if I'm stuck on a challenge? You can ask questions in the community forum or look for similar challenges.
  • How do I submit my solution? You can submit your solution using the "Submit" button on the challenge page.
  • Is there an official solution I can take a look at? Yes, after submitting your solution, you can check official solutions.
  • Do I get a code review when I post my solution? No, there is no official code review, but you can get feedback from the community.
  • Can I use libraries/frameworks on these projects? Yes, you can use any libraries or frameworks, but it’s encouraged to try solving the challenge without them.
  • Can I use these projects in my portfolio? Absolutely! Feel free to showcase them in your portfolio.
  • How often are new challenges added? New challenges are added regularly, typically every month.
  • What is the difference between the desktop-design, mobile-design, and desktop-preview images in my starter code? The desktop-design image is for larger screens, mobile-design is for smaller screens, and desktop-preview is a preview of the desktop layout.
  • Can I use these challenges within my own free or commercial content/tutorials/projects? Yes, you can incorporate them into your own projects as long as you credit Frontend Mentor.
